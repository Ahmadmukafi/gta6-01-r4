**DUSTLINE** is an open-world crime game set in Dustline City — a sun-baked desert
boomtown at golden hour, where the highway never ends and the storm always comes back.

You are **Rook**, the best getaway driver in the county. The fixer Marisol has five jobs
for you: a pickup at the Saguaro Motel, a getaway through the dust, a timed highway run,
a casino heist at The Mirage, and one last escape through the sandstorm to the airfield.

## What makes it different

- **DUST KICK (Q)** — the signature move. Kick a blinding cloud of desert dust in the
  sheriff's face: cruisers caught in it lose you, slow down, and weave blind. Charges
  refill over time, or instantly at the Gas-O-Line.
- **The sandstorm** — a living wall of dust rolls across the map every minute or so.
  Inside it the law can't see you and your heat drains twice as fast — but you can't
  see much either.
- **A town with names** — The Mirage Casino, Saguaro Motel, Dusty Spur Diner,
  Starlite Drive-In, Gas-O-Line, Dustline Airfield. Every place is a job, a hideout,
  or a trap.

## The world

- A full desert city: a street grid, a ring highway, an airstrip, dunes and cacti
  beyond the last streetlight
- Drive anything: steal cars off the street (the sheriff notices), traffic that cruises
  the grid, pedestrians on the sidewalks
- Wanted level 1–5: the sheriff's cruisers chase, ram, and shoot back at high heat
- Five story missions with dialogue, markers, timers, and cash rewards
- HUD with minimap (live storm circle, mission marker, police blips), cash, health,
  speed and dust charges — plus a start screen and automatic saving

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D / arrows | Move on foot / drive |
| Shift | Run |
| E | Steal / exit car |
| Q | Dust kick |
| Space | Handbrake (car) / jump (on foot) |
| Mouse click | Shoot the revolver |
| Mouse drag | Orbit camera |
| Mouse wheel | Zoom camera |
| P | Pause |
| M | Mute |
| R | Reset car |

## Play it

Serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome.
No build step, no network — every texture is drawn at runtime and every sound is
synthesized with WebAudio.
